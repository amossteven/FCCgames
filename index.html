How play-by-message works

1
Pick a game, type your name and your opponent's, and make your first move.
2
Tap Copy move. You get a one-line summary and a link. Paste it into the FCC chat.
3
Your opponent opens the link, sees your move, plays theirs, and pastes their link back.
4
Repeat until someone wins. Then hit Rematch — loser goes first.

The whole game lives inside the link, so nothing is stored anywhere and games never expire. Sound can be muted with the 🔊 button in each game.
